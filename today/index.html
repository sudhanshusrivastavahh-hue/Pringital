Lead · Weather

Cyclone Akvan Nears Coast; 72,000 Evacuated

BHUBANESWAR: Akvan has strengthened into a severe cyclonic storm over the Bay of Bengal and is likely to cross the coast between Narsapur in Andhra Pradesh and Puri in Odisha, close to Gopalpur, by Friday noon or afternoon. Winds of 90 to 100 km/h, gusting to 110, are expected as it makes landfall. About 72,000 people have been moved to safety across eastern India, and the Railways has rescheduled three trains. Kolkata and Gangetic West Bengal are under a heavy-rain warning, with very heavy rain likely at isolated places on Friday. Across the bay, about 1,000 tourists are stranded on Bangladesh’s St Martin’s Island as boats stay off the sea. ↗

Second Lead · Delhi–NCR

Delhi Air Slips Back to ‘Poor’ at 289

NEW DELHI: The capital’s AQI rose to 289 at 8:30 am on Thursday, up from 212 earlier in the morning, after averaging 232 on Wednesday and 204 on Tuesday. Pusa recorded 366, AIIMS 354 and Chandni Chowk 344. Forecasters warn of another pollution spell. The city has fined 25,610 vehicles and sealed 2,446 industrial units this month, and Chief Minister Rekha Gupta has ordered tighter checks at the borders. ↗
