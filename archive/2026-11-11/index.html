Lead · Diplomacy

Modi, Zelensky Talk Peace Ahead of Abu Dhabi Round

India weighs a role in restoring Black Sea grain routes; Jaishankar speaks to Lavrov the same day.

NEW DELHI: Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky spoke with Prime Minister Narendra Modi before renewed US-backed Russia–Ukraine peace negotiations in Abu Dhabi. The two discussed how India might contribute to the diplomatic effort and to reopening Black Sea grain-export routes, and India restated its support for a peaceful settlement. External Affairs Minister S. Jaishankar separately discussed the conflict with Russian Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov on Tuesday.

Cancer Drugs Get Cheaper: Savings Above ₹3,500 Crore a Year

NEW DELHI: Revised maximum retail prices for anti-cancer medicines took effect on 9 November under the government's trade-margin rationalisation. The National Pharmaceutical Pricing Authority picked 137 non-scheduled cancer drugs for the exercise, and more than 1,000 formulations have reported price cuts, with estimated consumer savings of over ₹3,500 crore a year.
