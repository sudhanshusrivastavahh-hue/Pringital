Lead · Economy

Inflation Hits 2-Year High of 6.09%, Breaks RBI Limit

NEW DELHI: Retail inflation rose to 6.09% in October from 5.54% in September, data released on Thursday showed, crossing the Reserve Bank’s 6% upper tolerance limit for the first time since October 2024. Food inflation climbed to 8.94% from 7.74% as vegetable and grain prices rose after a deficient monsoon. The reading sets the stage for the RBI’s monetary policy meeting on 2–4 December, where several economists expect a rate hike of 50 to 75 basis points over the coming months, which would push up loan EMIs. Growth remains firm, with SBI Research projecting 7.8% for the July–September quarter. DNFull story

Capital Braces for ‘Severe’ Air as AQI Heads to 428

NEW DELHI: Delhi’s air quality index stood at 314 on Thursday morning, and the Indian Institute of Tropical Meteorology expects it to reach 428, in the ‘severe’ band, by Friday. Weak winds, a falling mixing height and stubble smoke from neighbouring states are trapping pollutants over the city, while the share of firecrackers in PM2.5 has dropped to 7.4% from 29.8% on Tuesday. IAF opens AFCAT 1/2027 for 284 officer postsDN

NEW DELHI: The Indian Air Force has opened online applications for AFCAT 1/2027 to fill 284 Group ‘A’ officer posts in the Flying Branch and Ground Duty (Technical and Non-Technical). Registration began at 11 am on Thursday and closes at 11:30 pm on 11 December on afcat.edcil.co.in. The online exam is on 31 January 2027, followed by the Air Force Selection Board tests and a medical check. Age limits are counted as on 1 January 2028, when the courses begin, with entry through Permanent or Short Service Commission.
