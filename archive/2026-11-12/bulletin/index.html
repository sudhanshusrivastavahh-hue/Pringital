EconomyRetail inflation rose to six point zero nine per cent in October, from five point five four per cent in September. That is the highest in two years, and above the Reserve Bank’s upper limit of six per cent. Food inflation climbed to nearly nine per cent after a weak monsoon. The R.B.I.’s policy committee meets from the second to the fourth of December, and several economists expect interest rates to rise, which would push up loan E.M.I.s. DN

Delhi–NCRIn the capital, the air quality index stood at three hundred and fourteen this morning. Forecasters expect it to reach four hundred and twenty-eight, in the severe band, by Friday, as weak winds and stubble smoke trap pollution over the city. Schools in Noida and Ghaziabad have moved classes up to Class Five to hybrid mode, and Gurugram has banned coal and wood tandoors at eateries. DN
