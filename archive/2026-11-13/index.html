Exports Near $90 Billion; Goods Shipments Hit Record

NEW DELHI: India’s total exports of goods and services rose nearly 30% to $89.99 billion in October. Merchandise shipments alone touched a record $49.47 billion, despite the West Asia conflict.

Imports grew even faster, lifting the goods trade deficit to a one-year high of $39.88 billion, but a strong services surplus narrowed the overall gap to $20.09 billion. The Sensex fell 319 points a day after retail inflation hit 6.09%, as traders priced in a possible RBI rate hike on 4 December.↗

Second Lead · West Asia

Iran Says It Hit US Radar in Bahrain

Iran’s Revolutionary Guards said they targeted a US early-warning radar in Bahrain, home to the US Navy’s Fifth Fleet. They called it retaliation for a strike on a telecom tower in southern Iran.

Reuters called it Tehran’s first such claim in a Gulf country since early September. The IRGC Navy also said an LPG tanker blew up after hitting a sea mine in the Strait of Hormuz. Trump said Gulf allies had urged him to hold off big strikes, but he was not sure he agreed. Iran’s Speaker Ghalibaf said Tehran does not want the war to go on and the ‘ball is in America’s court’. For India, which ships much of its crude and LPG through the strait, Brent stays above $90.↗

On a Lighter Note

That Meow Is a Request: Cats Ask Humans for Help, Study Finds

Your cat may be meowing on purpose. Researchers at the University of São Paulo put a favourite treat in a box that 38 pet cats could smell but not reach.

With an attentive person in the room, the cats meowed 86 times. Left alone, they meowed just 20 times, so a cat was more than 16 times likelier to call out when someone was watching. The study, in Animal Behaviour, calls it ‘recipient-specific signalling’. The authors caution it does not prove cats know we can help. Cat owners may disagree.↗
