Lead · Sport

Dhoni Bows Out of the IPL, Becomes CSK Mentor

CHENNAI: MS Dhoni has retired from the Indian Premier League, ending a 278-match run in which he scored 5,439 runs with 24 fifties and 264 sixes, took 158 catches and led Chennai Super Kings to five titles. CSK named him team mentor from IPL 2027. Co-owner Rupa Gurunath said Dhoni raised his wish to retire during a call about retained players. He bid farewell with an Instagram reel set to ‘Main Har Ik Pal Ka Shayar Hoon’, the song he used when he quit international cricket in August 2020. His wife Sakshi called it a ‘beautiful new chapter’. Stars from Dhanush to Juhi Chawla said the game would not be the same without him. ↗

Second Lead · Delhi–NCR

GRAP-III Lifted, Schools Reopen as Air Eases

NEW DELHI: The air-quality commission has lifted Stage III curbs across Delhi–NCR, and schools have resumed physical classes for all students. The city’s AQI stood at 214, ‘poor’, at 7 am on Wednesday, down from 246 a day earlier and 382 on 6 November. Stages I and II stay in force, as do curbs on dusty construction and the 50% office-attendance rule until 20 November. Forecasters say air may swing between ‘poor’ and ‘very poor’. ↗
