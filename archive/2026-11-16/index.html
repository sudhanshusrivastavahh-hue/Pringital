The tour centres on the Earthshot Prize ceremony in Mumbai, to be hosted by Karan Johar, with cricket and Bollywood events on the side. ↗

Lead · Diplomacy

Modi in Manila for ASEAN, East Asia Summits

MANILA: Prime Minister Narendra Modi arrived in the Philippine capital on Monday for the 23rd India-ASEAN Summit and the 21st East Asia Summit, both on Tuesday. President Ferdinand Marcos Jr hosted a dinner for visiting leaders. Before it, Modi viewed the Laguna Copperplate Inscription, the oldest dated document found in the Philippines (around 900 CE), which records ancient links with India. The summits open under strain. ASEAN and China failed to finish a South China Sea code of conduct, though about 80% of the draft is agreed, and India restated its support for freedom of navigation. Indonesia’s President Prabowo Subianto apologised to Singapore and Malaysia over smoke from his country’s worst wildfires in 11 years. Whether Quad leaders will meet on the sidelines is still unclear. ↗

Second Lead · Delhi–NCR

Rain Brings Brief Relief; Smog Due Back by Tuesday

NEW DELHI: Delhi’s air improved to an average AQI of 213, in the ‘poor’ band, at 6 am on Monday, after stronger winds and light rain on Sunday. A day earlier it was 353, ‘very poor’. Sirifort (341) and NSIT Dwarka (331) were still ‘very poor’, while the Cantonment area read a ‘satisfactory’ 94. The early-warning system expects air to slip back to ‘very poor’ on 17–18 November and stay there for about six days. Biomass burning, including stubble fires, made up 37.1% of Sunday’s pollution. ↗
