How it works

Print it now, follow it later.

A morning paper is out of date by lunch. PRINGITAL NEWS is put together at the moment you ask for it, so the page always starts from the latest news.

  1. Choose your page

    Pick the sections you want: national, your city, markets, sport, weather. Your page is set up once.

  2. Print whenever you like

    Print on any home or office printer. The page carries a timestamp showing exactly when its news was current.

  3. Follow the live link

    Every brief has a code that opens the full story online, updated as it develops.
