Namaskar, and welcome to Pringital News. Credible like print, fast like digital. This is your four-minute bulletin for Sunday, the fifteenth of November, twenty twenty-six, from New Delhi.

First, the headlines. India have won the one-day series in New Zealand, a day after India’s under-nineteen team beat Pakistan to lift the Asia Cup. Four civilians have been killed in an ambush in Manipur. And Delhi celebrates Chhath under a hazy sky.

SportAt Mount Maunganui, India defended a modest two hundred and nine to beat New Zealand by twenty-two runs and win the series three–one. Arshdeep Singh took three wickets and Axar Patel scored forty-six and took two. It is India’s first away one-day series win under coach Gautam Gambhir. On Saturday, India’s under-nineteen team beat Pakistan by one wicket off the very last ball to win the Asia Cup, chasing three hundred and thirty-eight. Prime Minister Narendra Modi called it an outstanding day for Indian cricket.↗

ManipurManipur has ordered all security posts to tighten cover and step up combing operations, after four Naga civilians were killed in an ambush on the Imphal–Dimapur highway in Ukhrul district on Friday. The Tangkhul Naga Long has given the Centre and the state three days to act and wants the case handed to the N.I.A.↗

Delhi–NCRDelhi’s air quality index averaged three hundred and sixty-seven on Saturday, in the very poor band, after touching the severe level on Friday. Light rain is expected today, which may bring some relief. More than one thousand four hundred Chhath ghats are ready across the city, and Chief Minister Rekha Gupta will offer arghya at Sonia Vihar this evening.↗

NationPrime Minister Narendra Modi marks Janjatiya Gaurav Diwas, the birth anniversary of Bhagwan Birsa Munda, by opening and launching two hundred and forty-six tribal projects worth about three thousand crore rupees. Odisha has been sanctioned one lakh more rural homes, and Rajasthan’s board has called off its February exams because of the census.↗

WorldThe Kremlin says Donald Trump, Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin will meet in Shenzhen on the nineteenth of November. Iran’s Revolutionary Guard says two oil tankers hit mines near the Strait of Hormuz. And Israel says it has killed a top Hamas commander in southern Gaza.↗

BusinessThe Sensex ended the week at eighty-one thousand, five hundred and ninety-two, its third weekly gain in a row. Gold had its biggest weekly fall since June. And Air India is flying non-stop to New York again, saving up to five hours.↗

EntertainmentRamayana has set an all-time record for a second Saturday, and has crossed one thousand two hundred crore rupees worldwide. Panchayat season five will not release this year. And veteran Telugu actor Giri Babu has died in Hyderabad.↗

On a lighter noteSachin Tendulkar says he still has his very first bat, forty-nine years on. Tomorrow, twenty-six of his bats go on show in Mumbai, in an exhibition called Batography.↗
